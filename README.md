# Awesome App Launch

Free places to launch a new iPhone or Android app — with the rules that trip people up. Collected by hand while launching our own apps (last checked October 2026). Nobody paid to be on this list.

## Before you start
- A website or landing page, a privacy policy URL and a support URL.
- A one-line tagline under 60 characters, and 3–5 screenshots.
- Your store links. Most sites pull the name and icon from them.

## Listed right away
- [Bentavo](https://bentavo.com/submit) — Free. Paste an App Store link and the page is live at once; Android within a day. Shows how the app charges, with a free badge.
- [Get Indie Apps](https://getindieapps.com/submit) — Free, iOS and Android in one listing, no review.
- [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/add-product) — Free. List the app as the alternative to 3–5 well-known apps.
- [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com) — Android only. Claim your Play listing.
- [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com) — Free, approved in 2–7 days.
- [It's All Widgets](https://itsallwidgets.com) — Flutter apps only.

## Free launches that ask for a badge
- [TinyLaunch](https://www.tinylaunch.com) — Pick a free launch week; a person approves it.
- [Smol Launch](https://smollaunch.com) — Tags required; the free queue can be about 10 weeks.
- [OpenHunts](https://openhunts.com) — Add the badge to skip the queue.
- [Fazier](https://fazier.com) — New accounts may need a few real comments first.
- [Uneed](https://www.uneed.best) — One product in the free queue at a time; months long.
- [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io) — One launch at a time, weekly.
- [ScrollLaunch](https://www.scrolllaunch.com) — One product per domain.

## Launch days and communities
- [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) — Once per product. Tuesday–Thursday, 12:00 a.m. Pacific.
- r/SideProject and [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com) — "I built this" posts with the story.
- r/droidappshowcase — Android; r/androidapps bans self-promotion.
- r/AppHookup — Sales and limited-time free offers only.
- [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) — Only with a technical story.
- X, Threads, Bluesky — `#buildinpublic` `#indiedev` `#indieapp` and a short screen recording.

## By language
- **Korean** — 모두의 앱 (moduapp.kr), GeekNews "Show GN", Clien's self-promotion board only.
- **Japanese** — `#個人開発` on X; build-log posts on Zenn or note.

## Skip
- BetaList after launch — it is for pre-launch products.
- Topic subreddits that ban self-promotion. Answer questions there instead.

## Free tools
